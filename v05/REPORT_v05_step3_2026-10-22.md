   let me keep it."
3. **Q1 — a design result changed a parameter (disclosed):** at **1 question a day** curiosity changes nothing within
   120 nights (every rule ≤ 1/9 regions; differences are noise) — structurally, a 40-night window at founder pace
   (~0.5 mentions/region-night) holds ~20 outcomes while a verdict needs ~50. At **3 a day** (founder D3's maximum)
   decision-aware curiosity **roughly doubles what it understands**: +2.3…+5.2 SE over random and round-robin when
   the founder answers ≥ 50%; at 30% only by night 120. → its question may be asked in up to 3 conversations a day;
   Q1's bar is scoped to answer rates ≥ 50% (30% reported, not claimed).
4. **Q3 — free talk is untouched (Kaggle, pinned Part-2 env, real Ears v7):** on all 40 parity sentences v0.5's talk
   path equals v0.4's exactly; with a pending question it only adds the "asked" tag. **PASS.**

   identity with v0.4 10/10 in records and state. **PASS.** Live verify on the 6-day body life: 12/12 acts.
