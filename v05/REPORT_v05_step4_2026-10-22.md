## Findings and disclosures
1. **Limit found: proper names never reach the door.** 'komitas' is not tagged as a noun by the Ears, so it is never
   "unheard" — only unseen — and growth can never apply to it. Disclosed as a known limit of v0.5's word growth.
   (Run 1 assumed it would be at the door: a test fault, fixed by defining the test on door words.)
2. **The prediction's wording was imprecise and is now exact.** The gated Ears give each concept ONE mention per
   sentence, so a grown word can stand for its concept where another word shares it ("I heard a kamancheh at the
   concert": kamancheh now carries *music*, which concert carried before). No word's mapping changes, and the sentence
   hears the same concepts. The prediction now reads "the concept every other word is heard as" plus that note, and G1
   checks exactly that (concepts only gain; changes only in grown concepts). (Run 2 used the looser wording.)
3. **Tool safety:** looking at a proposal must not deliver a question — a read-only `status()` was added after I
   noticed the first version of `grow_v05.py` would have spent one of the day's questions.
