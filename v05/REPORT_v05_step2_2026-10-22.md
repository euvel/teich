## Findings
1. **D1 — a design claim failed, and was dropped (founder 10-21).** Thinking on region curves is strongly non-linear:
   cheap order-blind models miss by a median 49% (linear) to 115% (quadratic), versus an order effect of 14% — so
   "it discovers its thoughts do not commute" would have claimed its own crudeness as order. Claim 2 became: memory is
   knowable, chaos is given, **thinking is knowable only by thinking**; order sensitivity became an objective gate.
2. **The runtime geometry IS the genesis geometry:** rest rows rebuilt at run time equal v0.4's frozen table bit for bit.
3. **Its body reads its own thinking:** after 4 thoughts it read 1.599 vs exact 1.601; in the 3-day smoke 4.435 vs
   4.430, 3.302 vs 3.287, 6.176 vs 6.173; controls stayed at rest.
4. **Slot limit found by the smoke, fixed by law:** one realistic day needed 8 lifts (> v0.4's 7). 300 days need
   ≤ 11 faces / ≤ 9 lifts → 16/16 slots; padding proven inert (bit-identical dreams); last thoughts refused if ever
   exceeded. (My first slot check used only 3 days — too few; disclosed.)
5. **Gate O1, design run on Kaggle (36 pairs × 4 twins × 40,000 ticks):** single-dream noise 0.44%; 17 pairs above
   3× noise; **power 94%** (16/17 at ≥ 3 SE; strongest 47 SE); **false order 0/36**; **Spearman(order, crossing)
   +0.81** → PASS. Order matters, and more between unrelated thoughts.
6. **O2 in the real body (3 days, thinking on vs off):** main body, dials, heart and every non-thinking record (by
   meaning) identical. Thinking touches nothing it does not own.
7. **Its order-blind predictions missed on all 3 smoke nights** (3.15 vs 4.43, 1.94 vs 3.30, 4.33 vs 6.18) — the
   evidence claim 2 expects. Live verify: 6/6 acts pass, including its chosen thoughts.
8. **Cost:** one dream 13.4 s ≈ 12% of a day of the main body.
