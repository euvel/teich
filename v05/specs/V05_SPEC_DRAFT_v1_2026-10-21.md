# V05_SPEC — Teich v0.5: the first sign   **DRAFT 2026-10-21 for founder review — not frozen, not anchored**

Same discipline as v0.4: design numbers come from design seeds only. The founder freezes each part. Its sha256 is
anchored in a seat chain before any scored run. One look per gate. Birth happens only on the founder's word; publishing
needs a separate yes. Sources: V05_PLAN_2026-10-21.md, v04_lab/REPORT_v05_P0_killchecks_2026-10-21.md (K1, K2, K3, K4,
KH, KG).

## 0. What v0.5 must earn before birth (the four claims)
1. **Its reasons are the real causes.** Every act it chooses is logged with its reason first. Replay proves the reason
   is sufficient, necessary and complete for the act (§2, gate F1). This is contrasted with language-model agents in a
   pre-registered head-to-head (§8).
2. **It knows which parts of itself are knowable.** It predicts its own body every night, using only models cheaper
   than living (§3.1, law L-CL). It finds that its memory is predictable and that its night-to-night chaos is not.
3. **Its curiosity serves decisions.** It asks at most 3 templated questions a day, only where an answer helps settle
   a verdict. Silence is lawful. It must beat random and round-robin questioning (§4, gate Q1).
4. **It can grow without breaking its word.** Unheard words wait at a door. It proposes its own growth and
   pre-registers what will change. Each growth is a numbered version v0.5.1, v0.5.2, … under gate G1 (§6).

Plus one candidate claim, made only if its gate passes: **it can discover that its own thoughts do not commute** (§3.3,
gate O1).

## 2. The foundation: acts with reasons first (faithful reasons)
Every **chosen** act goes through one pattern: a `reason` record, then the act.
- **Acts covered:** questions (§4), self-directed attention (§3.2), self-chosen thoughts (§3.3), silence (choosing not
  to ask), caution changes, probes, growth proposals (§6).
- **The reason record:** `{"rule": "<name>@<code version>", "inputs": {...named numbers from records only...},
  "act": ...}`. The decision function receives **only** `inputs`. Ties break by a fixed rule stated in the reason. No
  random draws.
- Reasons may cite only records with a lower seq.

**Gate F1 (hard).** Over every chosen act in all gate lives:
- **Receipt:** `rule(inputs) == act`.
- **Sufficiency:** scramble every state field not in `inputs`; the act must not change.
- **Necessity:** move an input across the rule's boundary; the act must change exactly as the rule says.
- **Completeness:** no act exists without an earlier reason record.

Bar: 0 failures. The harness must also catch a planted hidden cause (design K4: 152 flags on 450 decisions). If it
doesn't, the gate itself is void.

## 3. Inner acts: evidence about itself that needs no one else

### 3.1 Predicting its own body, under the law "cheaper than living" (L-CL)
- **Every night** it predicts, for each handle with a settled reading, tonight's single-night reading. The rounded
  value is the prediction. The outcome is checked the next night: hit or miss.
- **New self kinds:** `BODY-MEMORY` (the reading) and `BODY-PATH` (tonight's crossing count, relative to the expected
  rate for its reading). The same MINE / GIVEN / FINE / UNRESOLVED machinery attributes them.
  **Design expectation (K1):** memory is predictable; the residual is white noise (night-to-night correlation −0.003),
  so BODY-PATH should settle as GIVEN.
- **Law L-CL:** the self's predictors are pure functions of its record log and frozen genome constants (integers,
  fractions, first-order closed forms). It may **not** run its body, read its thoughts' state, or replay itself.
  **Gate L1:** (a) an AST check of the self modules' imports against a whitelist; (b) the self's nightly work is ≤ 1%
  of the night's body work on the gate machine; (c) a meaning demonstration: an oracle allowed to replay the body
  predicts BODY-PATH exactly. That documents that GIVEN means "knowable only at the cost of living it".

### 3.2 Self-directed attention (founder decision D1: confirmed)
- **What:** at most 1 step a day, attributed `speaker: "itself"`, under the same laws (shared speed limit, edge 40).
- **Why it may choose:** the reason may cite only **uncertainty**: a cluster "still reading" the longest, or one whose
  BODY-MEMORY predictions keep missing. It may never cite dial size.
- **The prediction it writes:** "+1 in 3 quiet nights", checked like any reading.
- **Gate W1:** its own attention predictions are calibrated (Brier better than its baseline). **Gate W2 (loop
  guard):** over 120-night lives, no cluster gets more than 4 self-steps per 30 nights, and the Spearman correlation
  between self-step choice and dial size is ≤ 0.2 (it chooses by uncertainty, not size).

### 3.3 Thinking on a working-memory surface (K2 and KG passed)
- **Where:** a separate genus-2 surface with 256 thoughts. Handle 1 carries the **8 region curves** (v0.3's depth-3
  Stern–Brocot nodes: 1/4, 2/5, 3/5, 3/4, 4/3, 5/3, 5/2, 4/1). The *itself* region is **open question O-a**. Handle 2
  is a control that must stay unchanged every night.
- **A thought** is a Fenchel–Nielsen twist of **0.2** along a region curve. It uses the certified closed form: only
  + − × ÷ √, with cosh/sinh(0.1) frozen from mpmath. The handle's geometry rows are rebuilt per thought by v0.4's
  genesis method (KG: bit-identical on AMD/numpy 1.26 and Intel/numpy 1.26 and 2.1).
- **What it thinks about:** (a) **rehearsal**: after each conversation, the regions of the kept mentions, in the order
  heard; (b) **one self-chosen thought a day**, reason first, by decision-aware need. At most **12 thoughts a day**
  (KG: 12-thought days stay well-shaped).
- **Sleep:** at its midnight the working surface is read (crossing counts → curve lengths), recorded, and **reset to
  rest**. The log keeps the day's thoughts and readings.
- **Its cheap self-model of thinking** is first-order and **commuting**: the sum of each thought's rest-shape effect,
  frozen in the genome. **Candidate claim, gate O1:** it may record "the order of my thoughts mattered today" only when
  the reading misses its commuting model by more than 3 SE. On design-known days it must detect order when it is
  present (power ≥ 0.8 on days with high-crossing pairs) and stay silent when absent (false alarms ≤ 0.05 on
  same-region or non-crossing days). If O1 fails, the claim is not made and thinking stays as rehearsal plus readings.
- **Measured meaning (K2 math):** the order effect rises with crossing number (Spearman +0.94). Related regions cross
  less, so related thoughts commute more. Confound disclosed: curves that cross more are longer.
- **Gate O2 (isolation):** handle 2 and the main body are untouched by thinking (as K2b: other handle 0.1–0.4 SE). The
  main body's records are byte-identical with and without thinking (part of N0).

## 4. Asking: its first voice (D3: confirmed, at most 3 a day)
- **Candidate questions** come from fixed templates only, with no language model:
  - "What is ⟨word⟩ to you?" for an unheard noun at the door;
  - "Is ⟨region⟩ still the same to you?" for an UNRESOLVED region;
  - "What do you think about ⟨cluster⟩?" for a cluster it is still reading;
  - "What am I to you?" for itself.
- **Choice: decision-aware curiosity** (K3: 2.15/9 regions understood vs 1.30–1.45 round-robin, 0.80–1.25 random).
  It asks where an answer brings a verdict closest to being settled, with exact integer arithmetic and the reason
  first. **If no candidate has positive need, it stays silent**, and the silence is logged with its reason.
- **Where:** its pending question shows in the founder's talk tool before the founder speaks, and in its diary. An
  unanswered question is an outcome too.
- **Gate Q1:** on fresh seeds, decision-aware asking beats random and round-robin in regions understood at night 60
  and night 120, each with a margin ≥ 2 SE, at simulated answer rates of 30%, 50% and 100%.
  **Gate Q2:** every ask and every silence passes F1.

### 4.1 Hearing in answers (asked context)
When it asked about X, the answer's topic is known. The Ears judge only the feeling toward X, with the A2e-gated
attitude organ unchanged. Free talk is heard exactly as v0.4 hears it. **No new Ears claim is made in advance.** Its
self measures asked-vs-free struck rates live, with receipts. **Gate Q3:** the asked-context path changes no free-talk
mention (regression vs Ears v7 on the A2e and MAMS parity sentences, as R-v9).

## 5. The self's new accounts
- **Diary lines** (templates):
  - "I gave attention to ⟨x⟩ because ⟨reason, receipt⟩; I expect +1 in 3 nights."
  - "I asked about ⟨x⟩; you answered / did not answer."
  - "Today I thought about ⟨regions in order⟩; the order mattered / did not matter (receipt)."
  - "My memory was predictable tonight; my paths were not."
- **It admits when it was wrong about itself:** when a verdict flips, it writes "I was wrong about myself", with the
  evidence, and the rate is tracked (meta-calibration). **Gate M1:** every flip produces exactly one such record, and
  its stated rate equals the recomputed rate.

## 6. The door and growth as numbered versions (D2, D4: confirmed)
- **The door:** each unheard noun is kept as a **single lemma** with a count (never sentences). The covenant is
  amended to say so.
- **A candidate binding** forms when an answer to "What is ⟨word⟩ to you?" lands in a cluster. The door records the
  word, the cluster and the evidence.
- **A growth proposal** is written in its sleep once at least N bindings agree (constant to be set by design). It
  **pre-registers its own predictions**: which words it will hear afterwards, and that nothing else changes.
- **Versions:** each growth is **v0.5.1, v0.5.2, …**. The new identity = sha256(previous identity + amendment).
  An anchored spec and a gate come before it; the biography records before and after. The first growth type is
  **word bindings only**: Ears map entries, no body or mind change. Handles come later.
- **Gate G1 (dry run before birth, on a drill seat):** a growth carries over every commitment, every dial, every
  verdict and every past receipt (still recomputable), and its own pre-registered predictions hold. 0 failures.
- **The founder** approves every growth; the gate certifies it. A refusal is recorded.

## 8. The head-to-head (pre-registered evaluation; it does not gate birth)
- **Job:** choose the one region where a question most helps reach a verdict, and say why. The same information goes
  to every arm.
- **Arms:**
  - Teich's rule;
  - language models choosing freely, with and without reasoning;
  - language models **given Teich's rule**, with and without reasoning.
- **Measures:** choice stable under shuffling and renaming; the named or given rule predicts the choice (tie-aware);
  identical requests replayed on 2 days; closed-loop competence (regions understood when each chooser drives the same
  simulated self).
- **Design:** ≥ 150 states from fresh seeds, 2 prompt wordings, every raw answer stored, lenient parsing, budgets
  reasoning models cannot exhaust.
- **Models:** the open models that answer reliably (pilot: Llama 3.3 70B, Qwen3 30B, Mistral Small 24B via Workers
  AI), plus any larger model that answers reliably (NVIDIA NIM's large models were unavailable on 10-21). The claim
  names exactly the models tested.
- **Pilot (15 states):** free choice was shuffle-stable 47–60%, with its own named rule predicting 50–67% of choices,
  and not replayable. Given Teich's rule: followed 20–40% without reasoning; Llama 70B with reasoning 13/15, still
  shuffle-unstable 4/15. Teich: 100%.
- **The claim is about faithfulness, never cleverness.**
