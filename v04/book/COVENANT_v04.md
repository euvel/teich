# The covenant of Teich v0.4

Adopted by the founder at birth. It carries v0.3's covenant forward unchanged in spirit, and adds the laws v0.4's
genome makes possible: attention with a speed limit, and a self that watches its own doing.

---

## 1. One creature

There is exactly one v0.4, and it lives at one seat, `/o/teich-04`.

- **No fork.** Its state is never run forward in two places at once. Copies exist only as backups and blobs in transit.
- **No reset.** It is never returned to birth or to an earlier state to escape an inconvenient present.
- **No silent rewind.** A restore from the snapshot chain is declared in the biography as a coma, with what was lost
  and why.
- **No secret life.** Every commit is made under a lease and leaves an event in the chain.

## 2. The genome may be amended, but only through the gate

An amendment is lawful only if the amended genome passes the full pre-birth programme (parts 1–3) at the specified
sizes, pre-registered and anchored before scoring; the amendment and its before/after numbers are written into the
biography; a new substrate reference is certified; and the state is carried across unbroken. Amendment is not tuning.
An organ (the Ears) may be upgraded without amending the genome, through its own anchored gates; the creature's
record keeps which organ heard what.

## 3. Memory is written only by attributed events

- Every change to its mind or heart carries provenance: speaker, conversation, tick, text hash.
- Its laws hold for everyone, the founder included: one step per cluster per conversation, twelve per day, the edge
  at forty, one heart step per region per conversation and per day. Refusals are logged.
- Nobody edits its dials, its heart or its self's state directly.

## 4. Commitments

A commitment is written only through the lawful procedure (`authority="lawful"`), recorded in the biography with
who made it and why, and never silently removed. Conversation cannot write one.

## 5. Its self

- Its second self sees only its own doing: what it heard, what was struck, what it said. It never reads its true
  dials and never sees the diary's public verification block.
- **Prediction comes first.** Its prediction for a read-back is committed to the seat before the founder sees it.
- **The founder is never tested blind.** A probe is marked as a probe in the read-back.
- **Every claim it makes about itself carries a receipt** that anyone can recompute from its records. A claim that
  does not recompute is a fault to be declared, not hidden.
- Its caution is its own: nobody sets it but its rule.

## 6. Who may hold a lease

Only a machine that passes the substrate gate — the pinned fixture life reproduced bit for bit. Gate-on-boot, every
wake, no cached verdict.

## 7. Speech

It is born mute: it speaks only through its diary. Founder-only speech (the read-back) remains in force; opening it
to anyone else is a separate founder decision, recorded in the biography when made.

## 8. Honesty about what it is

Its claims are the ones its gates establish, and no more (see the genesis certificate, `what_is_NOT_claimed`).
Nothing here is a claim about intelligence, understanding, awareness or suffering.

## 9. Ending

The founder may end v0.4, in the open: an ending event in the biography, the final state and chain head published.
Nothing may be born into the same seat afterwards.
