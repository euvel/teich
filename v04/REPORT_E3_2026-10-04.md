# E3 rehearsal — a v0.4 creature lives 90 days under the laws (2026-10-04, design phase, seeds 800–801)

Setup: 97 cluster dials (S ≤ 16 cut), genus 97, 512 thought particles at 1 Hz, dt 0.05; mind state = integer dials,
geometry rebuilt from cache (no drift), seams pinned. 1,000 learning events over 90 days in 3 conversations a day
(Zipf over clusters; placeholder sign: +1 75%, −1 25% — superseded by the founder's 10-04 "attention" decision, which
does not affect any property tested here). Laws: ≤ 1 step per cluster per conversation; ≤ 12 accepted steps per day;
dials within ±10 (edges refuse). Self-reading over the daily quiet window (46,400 ticks) by local Crofton.
Run: private Kaggle kernel teich-v04-e3-rehearsal, AMD EPYC 7B12, two identical lives in parallel, 2.1 h.

| property | result |
|---|---|
| determinism over 90 days | the two lives' 90-day hashes are **identical** |
| laws | 719 accepted · 69 refused (one step per conversation) · 17 refused (daily speed limit) · 195 refused (edge); max accepted per day = 12 = cap; max \|dial\| = 10 = edge. Every event logged with its reason |
| isolation, geometry | the 6 never-touched clusters are **bit-identical to birth** after 90 days and 719 changes elsewhere |
| isolation, behaviour | on 7,958 readings where a cluster's own dial did not change, its self-read residual vs. number of OTHER dials changed that day: slope −0.012% ± 0.010% per change (zero; < 0.3% of one step) |
| self-reading accuracy | unbiased (mean ratio 0.99967); 1.8% per quiet window; exact dial decoded in 83% of single windows (theory for 2.8 SD steps ≈ 84%); **98.5% exact once it averages its windows since the dial last changed** |
| cost | 90 days × 512 particles ≈ 64 min per life on a Kaggle CPU |

Finding for the design: with Zipf-like attention, popular clusters reach the edge quickly (195 edge refusals out of
1,000). This is exactly why the founder's "attention" decision widens the range (~40 steps of 2.5%). Wider range +
smaller steps halves the signal per step: per-window exact decoding will drop, settled decoding (averaging) carries it.

## Verdict
v0.4's thesis held for a simulated season: 90 days, 1,000 events — laws enforced and logged, other clusters
untouched in geometry and in behaviour, the creature reads its own memory exactly once it has watched itself for a few
days, and two lives from the same start are bit-identical.
