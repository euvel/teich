# K12 — the pants-local body at scale (2026-10-03, design phase, seeds 760–789)

Engine (body_pants.py): closed genus-g surface = g handles (clusters) hanging off a trivalent tree of g−2 pants.
Each piece has its own frame and a small fundamental polygon of its convex core (handle: 8 pair + 2 seam faces;
pants: 4 pair + 4 seam faces; both area exactly 2π). A particle carries (piece, p, v); per tick it checks only its
piece's ≤ 10 faces; crossing a seam maps it into the neighbour's frame. Tick element-wise (+ − × ÷ √) as in K1.

Bug found and fixed on the way: handle–pants seams were glued with the wrong orientation (particles bounced between two
pieces forever); seam maps now CHECK which orientation puts one piece's outside inside the other.

Deterministic (identical hashes). Cross-machine bit-identity of THIS engine not yet run (same arithmetic style as K1,
which matched AMD vs Intel).

## Cost — flat in genus
1.20 / 1.14 / 1.10 µs per particle-tick at genus 8 / 64 / 128 (2,000 particles). One day of life (86,400 ticks) of a
genus-128 creature with 512 particles: **36 s** on the laptop.

## Readability at genus 128 (512 particles, 10 independent days, one dial changed by an exact +10.5%)
| estimator | per-dial SD in one day | levels (ℓ 1–6, 5 SD) | +10.5% detected |
|---|---|---|---|
| global Crofton (crossings / total time) | 6.1% (slow mixing across the tree) | ~6 | 3 / 10 days |
| **local Crofton (crossings / time spent in the cluster)** | **1.5%** | **~23** | **10 / 10 days** (read +11.2%) |

## Verdict
A genus-128 creature (≈128 independent, exactly isolated memory dials) lives a day in about half a minute, and reads
each dial to ~23 levels per day by counting its thoughts inside each cluster. Scale knob: particles (cost linear,
SD ∝ 1/√particles). Open: cross-machine run of this engine; dial changes at birth-scale curriculum (E3);
pinning seam twists when a dial moves; bigger dt.
