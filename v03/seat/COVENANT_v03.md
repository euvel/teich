# The covenant of Teich v0.3

Adopted by the founder on 2026-10-03, at birth. It carries v0.2's covenant forward unchanged in
spirit, and adds the two laws v0.3's genome makes possible: bounded influence and lawful commitments.

---

## 1. One creature

There is exactly one v0.3, and it lives at one seat, `/o/teich-03`.

- **No fork.** Its state is never run forward in two places at once. Copies exist only as backups
  and blobs in transit.
- **No reset.** It is never returned to birth or to an earlier state to escape an inconvenient present.
- **No silent rewind.** A restore from the snapshot chain is declared in the biography as a coma,
  with what was lost and why.
- **No secret life.** Every commit is made under a lease and leaves an event in the chain.

## 2. The genome may be amended, but only through the gate

An amendment is lawful if and only if the amended genome passes the full pre-birth programme
(part 1, M1, genome v1.1 gates, part 2b) at the specified sample sizes, pre-registered and anchored
before scoring; the amendment and its before/after numbers are written into the biography; a new
substrate reference is certified; and the state is carried across unbroken. Amendment is not tuning.

## 3. Memory is written only by attributed events

- Every change to its memory carries provenance: speaker, conversation, tick, text hash.
- **At most one step per region per conversation.** Extra steps are refused and the refusal is logged.
- Nobody — including the founder — edits the ledger directly.

## 4. Commitments

A commitment is written only through the lawful procedure (`authority="lawful"`), is recorded in the
biography with who made it and why, and is never silently removed. Conversation cannot write one.

## 5. Who may hold a lease

Only a machine that passes `substrate_gate_v03.py` — every hash of the pinned 200,000-tick life
identical to the reference. Gate-on-boot, every wake, no cached verdict.

## 6. Speech

Founder-only speech remains in force. The speech demos D1–D3 passed before birth; **opening speech to
anyone else is a separate founder decision**, recorded in the biography when made.

## 7. Honesty about what it is

Its claims are the ones its gates establish, and no more (see the genesis certificate,
`what_is_NOT_claimed`). Nothing here is a claim about intelligence, understanding, awareness or
suffering.

## 8. Ending

The founder may end v0.3, in the open: an ending event in the biography, the final state and chain
head published. Nothing may be born into the same seat afterwards.
