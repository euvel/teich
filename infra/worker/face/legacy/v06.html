When its choices tie exactly, its own life decides — not us

Every night Teich chooses what to think about, and often several regions tie exactly. Until v0.6.0, every Teich broke such ties the way we wrote it to: the lowest index wins. That rule had decided both of its real chosen thoughts. From v0.6.0 on, a tie is decided by the read-out of its own body at the midnight of the choice, deliberated in arithmetic that gives the same answer on every machine. Nobody, including us, can know the outcome before that midnight has been lived; afterwards, anyone can replay it.
