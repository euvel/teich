The book is open: the repository is public and its history is verifiable — the diary can be shown to have been written when it says it was, one day at a time, and every published figure traces to the artifact and commit that produced it. Open book is not open speech. Founder-only speech remains in force for all four creatures. v0.3’s speech tests passed before birth; its speech opens only after live maturity gates, pre-registered in the same way, pass. Nothing here lets a stranger talk to any of them, and nothing on this page was written by a language model. No creature’s private phase has ever left its seat unencrypted (v0.3, v0.4 and v0.5 have none: nothing in them is hidden).
